How to Claim a Bonus

The procedure is the same for all four offers.

  1. Log in (register first for the welcome bonus);
  2. Open the promotions section and read the card of the offer;
  3. Activate the offer if the card requires it;
  4. Go to the cashier and deposit the qualifying amount;
  5. Check the balance: the bonus funds appear according to the terms.

If the bonus does not appear, contact support before playing with the deposit, because starting to play may void the claim.

Terms to Check Before Claiming

The percentage alone does not tell you whether an offer suits the way you play; these lines in the card do.

  • wagering requirement: how many times the bonus (or deposit + bonus) must be bet before withdrawal;
  • eligible games and their contribution percentages;
  • maximum bonus amount and maximum bet while the bonus is active;
  • validity period for meeting the wagering;
  • whether one bonus must be completed before the next is claimed.

These vary per offer and can be updated, so the card inside the account is the source of truth; this page describes the offers as published in July 2026.
